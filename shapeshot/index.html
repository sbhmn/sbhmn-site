Shapeshot

Pool and snooker on real physics. Six games, a CPU ladder and run-out puzzles.

Join the closed test

The game

Real physics

Spin, throw, squirt and a real cushion model. The lines you see are the engine's own prediction.

Six games

8-ball, 9-ball, 10-ball, Blackball, Snooker and Six-Red, on the official rules.

Make %

Every shot shows how often a player at your level makes it.

48 CPU players

A rated ladder, ranked matches with an Elo rating per game. Offline, no account.

Puzzles

Run-outs in three levels: Hard, Very Hard and Ultra.

Free to play

Ads between racks and optional in-app purchases. No coins, no energy, no loot boxes. Looks never change a shot.
